I am a Ph.D. student in Aerospace Engineering at Seoul National University, working in the Lab for Autonomous Robotics Research (LARR) under the supervision of Prof. H. Jin Kim. I received my B.S. in Mechanical & Aerospace Engineering from Seoul National University in 2025.

I approach aerial manipulation from two directions. On the control side, I develop controllers for omnidirectional aerial platforms and contact-rich aerial tasks, such as robust tiltrotor control under sudden disturbances and force-regulated sliding along unknown curved surfaces. On the autonomy side, I study how embodied coding agents and vision-language models can turn natural-language instructions into reliable, multi-stage aerial manipulation behaviors that transfer from simulation to real hardware.

Highlights

Embodied coding agents often fail on aerial manipulators because limited onboard sensing hides task outcomes and fragile physical interaction obscures why a program failed. We introduce Active Feedback, where the agent writes diagnostic programs that run in simulation to look from informative viewpoints (View) or test failure hypotheses through targeted interaction (Probe). Across six simulated tasks, this raises task success from 31.7% to 75%, and the refined programs run on a real aerial manipulator without source-code changes.

A perception-and-control pipeline that fits a superquadric model to an onboard point cloud, extracts contact normals and sliding trajectories in closed form, and tracks them with a hybrid motion/force controller combining a switching disturbance observer and RISE-based force control.

A kinematic-slip-aware path-planning framework that lets multiple nonholonomic robots carry a large object through cluttered environments while maintaining rigid formation.
